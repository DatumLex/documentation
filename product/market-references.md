# Market References — DatumLex

## Purpose

This document records external products and public materials used as references
during DatumLex product discovery. A reference can inspire questions, product
structure, or evaluation criteria; it does not define DatumLex requirements and
does not authorize copying protected text, visual identity, assets, source code,
data, or proprietary behavior.

## Reference — Veredictum

- Website: [Veredictum](https://veredictumapp.com.br/)
- Reviewed on: 2026-09-14.
- Reference type: Brazilian legal-technology product and positioning benchmark.
- Observed public structure: the website separates its product ecosystem by
  audiences and describes a distinct need and value proposition for each group,
  including legal education, the professional legal market, and examination
  candidates.

### Lessons considered for DatumLex

- State the audience before listing product capabilities.
- Connect every audience to a concrete problem and expected outcome.
- Separate educational and professional contexts when their data, risk, or
  workflow differs.
- Present a coherent product family or direction without making every possible
  legal workflow part of the current scope.
- Use clear language to distinguish academic, simulated, and professional use.

### DatumLex interpretation

DatumLex applies those lessons to its own purpose: transparent analytics from
public judicial data for evidence-informed legal research, professional
analysis and institutional understanding. Its current dashboard,
data model, methodology, visual identity, architecture, wording, and backlog are
independent DatumLex work governed by this repository.

Veredictum offerings shown publicly—such as academic assessment, simulations,
case or deadline management, certificate workflows, and examination
preparation—are not automatically DatumLex features. Similarity within the
broad legal-technology domain is not evidence of a validated DatumLex need.

## Reference — Juri Analytics

- Website: [Juri Analytics](https://jurianalytics.com/)
- Reviewed on: 2026-09-14.
- Reference type: direct Brazilian legal analytics and jurimetrics benchmark.
- Observed public focus: transforming case-law data into strategic intelligence
  through thesis mapping, decision trends, and judicial-profile analysis for law
  firms and legal departments.

### Lessons considered for DatumLex

- Connect statistical indicators to a concrete legal research question.
- Let users compare trends while retaining the underlying legal context.
- Explain how classification, coverage, and source quality affect conclusions.
- Treat decision profiles as a sensitive analytical feature requiring strong
  methodology and safeguards.

### DatumLex interpretation

Juri Analytics is close to the long-term DatumLex legal analytics proposition.
DatumLex initially differentiates itself through a narrow, documented DataJud
scope, explicit unavailable-data states, reproducible methodology, and combined
professional legal decision-support goals.

## Reference — Turivius

- Website: [Turivius Jurimetrics](https://turivius.com/jurimetria/)
- Reviewed on: 2026-09-14.
- Reference type: direct Brazilian jurisprudential research, AI, and
  jurimetrics benchmark.
- Observed public focus: searching a large decision collection, extracting
  structured information, displaying success rates and trends, comparing courts
  and adjudicating bodies, and exporting analytical reports.

### Lessons considered for DatumLex

- Join legal search and quantitative analysis in one understandable workflow.
- Present the path from a legal thesis to selected decisions, structured data,
  charts, and a usable report.
- Make comparisons and time trends easy to interpret.
- Consider export only when methodology and scope metadata travel with the
  result.

### DatumLex interpretation

Turivius is a useful benchmark for professional workflows and analytical UX.
Competitor predictive language and commercial scale are not DatumLex commitments.
The client-aligned roadmap does include grounded narrative drafts and exports in
Sprint 3, built on validated descriptive indicators and real-data reconciliation.

## Reference — Data Lawyer Insights

- Website: [Data Lawyer](https://www.datalawyer.com.br/)
- Product help: [Jurimetrics documentation](https://ajuda.datalawyer.com.br/pt-br/jurimetria)
- Reviewed on: 2026-09-14.
- Reference type: direct Brazilian judicial big-data and jurimetrics benchmark.
- Observed public focus: collecting and structuring judicial records for
  statistical research, legal research, case-group analysis, outcome and
  duration indicators, and chart exports.

### Lessons considered for DatumLex

- Offer filters and analytical dimensions that map to real professional
  questions.
- Explain discrepancies between a user's case population and displayed totals.
- Support reproducible groups or scopes rather than isolated chart screenshots.
- Treat collection coverage, update frequency, and process completeness as
  product information.

### DatumLex interpretation

Data Lawyer is a strong benchmark for data coverage, filters, and professional
analysis. DatumLex currently relies on an approved official-source scope and
does not claim equivalent collection scale, process-management features, or
predictive capability.

## Reference — Lex Machina

- Website: [Lex Machina by LexisNexis](https://www.lexisnexis.com/en-us/products/lex-machina.page)
- Reviewed on: 2026-09-14.
- Reference type: direct international litigation analytics benchmark.
- Observed public focus: converting litigation documents into structured data
  about case outcomes, findings, damages, timing, parties, counsel, judges, and
  courts, including appellate analytics and API access.

### Lessons considered for DatumLex

- Connect aggregate analytics with the source events and documents that explain
  them.
- Treat timing, outcome, court, and appeal dimensions as separate analytical
  concepts.
- Design for several professional questions without hiding data provenance.
- Use domain review and structured-data quality controls before presenting
  derived legal findings.

### DatumLex interpretation

Lex Machina is the clearest international reference for a mature legal analytics
category. DatumLex should learn from its structured analytical model and
question-oriented workflows without adopting unsupported predictions, entity
rankings, proprietary terminology, or US-specific legal assumptions.

## Reference — Bloomberg Law Litigation Analytics

- Website: [Bloomberg Law](https://pro.bloomberglaw.com/products/legal-research-and-software/)
- Reviewed on: 2026-09-14.
- Reference type: adjacent international research and litigation analytics
  ecosystem benchmark.
- Observed public focus: integrating legal sources, docket research, practical
  guidance, news, workflow tools, and analytics concerning courts, judges,
  attorneys, law firms, and companies.

### Lessons considered for DatumLex

- Analytics becomes more useful when users can move into deeper legal research.
- A consistent ecosystem can reduce context switching between discovery,
  interpretation, and communication.
- Product navigation should distinguish source material, analytical insight,
  guidance, and generated assistance.

### DatumLex interpretation

Bloomberg Law is broader than DatumLex and operates as a commercial research
suite. It is relevant to long-term information architecture, but it should not
drive the initial feature scope or imply that DatumLex supplies equivalent legal
content or professional guidance.

## Institutional Reference — CNJ Codex

- Website: [CNJ Codex Platform](https://www.cnj.jus.br/sistemas/plataforma-codex/)
- Reviewed on: 2026-09-14.
- Reference type: Brazilian institutional data architecture and public-justice
  intelligence reference; not a commercial competitor.
- Observed public focus: consolidating structured procedural data and document
  text so authorized applications can support business-intelligence dashboards,
  unified search, statistics, and artificial-intelligence initiatives.

### Lessons considered for DatumLex

- Separate source consolidation from the analytical applications that consume
  it.
- Preserve lineage between judicial systems, structured data, documents, and
  derived indicators.
- Design analytical services around governed, reusable data foundations.

### DatumLex interpretation

Codex is most useful as an architecture and governance reference. DatumLex uses
DataJud within its currently approved scope and must not imply privileged access
to Codex, institutional endorsement, or equivalent national coverage.

## Institutional Reference — CNJ Atalaia

- Website: [CNJ Atalaia](https://www.cnj.jus.br/tecnologia-da-informacao-e-comunicacao/atalaia/)
- Reviewed on: 2026-09-14.
- Reference type: Brazilian institutional process-pattern analysis reference;
  not a commercial competitor.
- Observed public focus: applying artificial intelligence and procedural-data
  analysis to identify patterns and similarities involving cases, parties,
  lawyers, and procedural content across courts and justice branches.

### Lessons considered for DatumLex

- Pattern detection can support institutional intelligence beyond a single
  dashboard metric.
- Similarity and entity analysis require clear authorization, purpose,
  explainability, privacy protection, and bias review.
- Public-sector legal analytics must prioritize accountability and safeguards.

### DatumLex interpretation

Atalaia informs future research questions, not the current DatumLex commitment.
DatumLex does not currently promise cross-court similarity detection, party or
lawyer profiling, fraud detection, or institutional monitoring.

## Cross-reference findings

Across the benchmark set, the most relevant recurring product capabilities are:

- a clearly defined audience and legal question;
- court, subject, period, outcome, procedural, and organizational filters;
- outcome rates, volume, timing, trends, and comparisons;
- navigation from aggregate insight to supporting decisions or documents;
- visible source coverage, methodology, exclusions, and update information;
- export or sharing that preserves analytical context;
- structured data and quality controls behind visual analytics; and
- safeguards around predictions, profiles, personal data, and AI-generated
  conclusions.

These findings are discovery inputs. They become DatumLex requirements only
after target-user validation, Product Owner prioritization, technical and legal
assessment, and creation of explicit backlog items.

## Benchmarking rules

When adding or reviewing a market reference:

1. Link the public source and record the access or review date.
2. Describe observed facts separately from team interpretation.
3. Identify the user problem or product question being investigated.
4. Record what may inform DatumLex and what remains outside scope.
5. Validate the lesson with target users and project constraints.
6. Create backlog work only after Product Owner review and prioritization.
7. Produce independent wording, interaction, visual design, architecture, code,
   and assets.
8. Respect trademarks, licenses, terms, privacy, copyright, and other applicable
   rights.

Do not use a competitor screenshot as a final DatumLex specification. Preserve
screenshots only when necessary for legitimate internal research, with source,
date, purpose, and access constraints recorded.
