# Product Roadmap — DatumLex

## Purpose

This roadmap connects the Product Vision to outcome-oriented delivery horizons.
It is intentionally directional and contains no invented dates or guarantees.
The [GitHub Product Backlog](https://github.com/orgs/DatumLex/projects/1) remains
the live source for sprint scope, priority, ownership, dates, and status.

Update this document when validated learning changes the product direction. Do
not use it as a second operational backlog.

## Roadmap principles

- Prioritize validated user value and trustworthy data before feature volume.
- Complete thin, integrated outcomes rather than isolated layers that cannot be
  evaluated by users.
- Keep methodology, accessibility, security, and evidence within the scope of
  delivery.
- Expand courts, subjects, and audiences only after the current analytical path
  is reliable and understandable.
- Use user research and observed product behavior to revise later horizons.

## Now — Validate the analytical foundation

### Desired outcome

Demonstrate a reproducible path from official DataJud records to an accessible
legal analytics dashboard for the initial TJDFT Civil Liability scope.

### Product capabilities

- Validate source access, feasibility, scope, and metric methodology.
- Define the dimensional model, data dictionary, classification rules, and
  traceable data pipeline.
- Expose documented merit indicators and filters through an API.
- Present analyzed appeals, granted and denied outcomes, and G1/G2 context in a
  responsive dashboard.
- Show honest loading, error, empty, unavailable, and partial-data states.
- Apply the approved DatumLex visual identity and accessibility baseline.
- Establish repeatable development, review, QA, deployment, and evidence
  practices.

### Exit evidence

- Dashboard values reconcile with the API and validated source data.
- Source, denominator, exclusions, classification, limitations, and refresh
  information are visible and documented.
- Independent review, automated checks, deployment, smoke testing, and Product
  Owner acceptance are recorded for the integrated scope.
- Representative users can interpret the principal view without mistaking
  historical patterns for predictions.

## Next — Improve exploration and user understanding

### Desired outcome

Enable priority audiences to answer broader legal research questions while
preserving traceability and responsible interpretation.

### Candidate outcomes for validation

- Improve drill-down from aggregate indicators to relevant source decisions.
- Add approved analytical dimensions and comparisons based on user research.
- Provide clearer methodology, coverage, refresh, and data-quality explanations
  inside the product.
- Support saved or shareable analytical contexts without exposing sensitive
  information.
- Improve exports or API access with the metadata required for reproducible
  research.
- Test audience-specific onboarding for legal professionals, students, and
  researchers.
- Strengthen automated accessibility, UI, API, data-quality, and regression
  coverage.

These are candidates, not commitments. Each requires evidence, refinement,
readiness, prioritization, and explicit backlog items.

## Later — Expand validated legal intelligence

### Desired outcome

Extend the platform to additional legal domains and institutional uses only when
the product can maintain reliable data, understandable methodology, and
responsible safeguards.

### Opportunities to investigate

- Additional courts, subjects, time ranges, and judicial result types.
- Comparative and longitudinal analyses across validated dimensions.
- Research workspaces for academic or institutional studies.
- Carefully governed text-analysis or classification assistance.
- Organization-level collaboration and reporting capabilities.
- Audience-specific educational experiences and methodology learning.

Before adoption, investigate source authorization, data quality, bias, privacy,
security, accessibility, operational cost, and the risk of misleading users.
DatumLex must not become an automated verdict or success-prediction system.

## Explicitly outside the current commitment

- Guaranteed case-outcome prediction.
- Automated legal advice or replacement of professional judgment.
- Ranking judges, parties, lawyers, or courts without an approved and ethically
  reviewed purpose.
- Case management, deadline control, electronic filing, or digital certificate
  management.
- Expansion based only on competitor feature parity.
- Production claims based on fixtures, illustrative data, or an unintegrated UI.

An item may move into scope later only through evidence, Product Owner ordering,
team refinement, and the normal development process.
