# Team Permanence Rule — DatumLex

## Purpose

To maintain team organization, commitment, and participation, DatumLex follows
a three-strike rule.

A strike may be assigned when a member repeatedly fails to comply with
established project responsibilities or rules.

## Applying a strike

A strike is applied when one of the situations described below occurs and the
member does not provide a valid justification. The situation must first be
discussed with the member. The Product Owner and Scrum Master confirm the strike
together.

After confirmation, the member's name, date, accumulated strike number,
objective reason, and the people who applied it must be added to the
[Strike Register](strike-register.md).

## When a strike may be assigned

Examples include:

- repeatedly failing to complete assigned tasks without justification;
- consistently failing to participate in Scrum activities;
- ignoring established repository or documentation rules;
- repeatedly submitting work that does not follow agreed standards;
- failing to meet agreed project responsibilities;
- prolonged inactivity without notifying the team; or
- other actions that significantly affect the team's progress.

## Three-strike system

| Strikes | Status |
|---:|---|
| 0 | Regular participation |
| 1 | First warning |
| 2 | Final warning |
| 3 | Maximum number of strikes reached |
| More than 3 | Member may be removed from the project group |

A member who exceeds three strikes may be removed from the project group.

Temporary unavailability communicated in advance through the team WhatsApp
group will not result in a strike for the communicated period.

The purpose of this rule is not to punish legitimate difficulties, but to
ensure accountability, communication, and commitment to the project.
