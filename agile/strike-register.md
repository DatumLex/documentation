## Registration rules

- Add one row for each applied strike.
- Use the member's name as listed in the project documentation.
- Use the date format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
- Record the accumulated strike number after the new occurrence.
- Describe the reason objectively and relate it to the Team Permanence Rule.
- Identify the Product Owner and Scrum Master who confirmed the strike.
- Do not remove or change an existing record without documenting the correction
  in the same Pull Request or commit.

## Entry template

```markdown
| <member> | <YYYY-MM-DD> | <strike number> | <objective reason> | <PO and Scrum Master> |
```
