# 📖 Manuals

This folder holds user-facing and operational manuals for **DatumLex** — documentation written for people who will *use* or *run* the system, as opposed to people building it.

## Contents

- **User manual** — how a legal professional or researcher uses the dashboard: logging in, applying filters (by theme, court, author, period), reading precedent trend charts, exporting results.
- **API documentation** — available endpoints, request/response formats, authentication, and usage examples for anyone integrating with DatumLex programmatically. *(Explicit non-functional requirement from the challenge.)*
- **Installation / setup guide** *(if applicable)* — how to run the project locally or in a given environment (dependencies, environment variables, seed data).
- **Data source manual** *(optional)* — a plain-language explanation of where the data comes from (DataJud/CNJ, state TJs, official gazettes) and any known limitations, for non-technical stakeholders.

## Why it matters

These manuals are what makes the system usable and adoptable by someone outside the development team — including the Xertica partner contacts and the course evaluators, who need to understand how to operate DatumLex without reading the source code.
