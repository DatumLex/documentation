# Source Research — DatumLex

Research notes record examples and hypotheses; they do not prove deployed source
integration, current endpoint availability, full-text coverage, or reuse rights.

| Note | Planning boundary |
|---|---|
| [DataJud](api-data-ajude.md) | Only Sprint 1 source; examples outside TJDFT/Civil Liability/2023 onward are historical research, not the production query. |
| [TJDFT jurisprudence](api-jurisprudencia-do-tjdft.md) | Input for later text-source feasibility; not a Sprint 1 connector or proof of complete text. |
| [Pangea](api-pangea.md) | Candidate precedent research; not a committed doctrine feed or Sprint 1 source. |

Sprint 2 prioritizes STJ and approved open doctrine, especially BDJur. BDTD and
SciELO are client-suggested candidates requiring individual feasibility/rights
review. Link reproducible access evidence, query scope, timestamps, limits,
coverage, rights, and constraints before implementation. Do not infer full text
or successful outcome classification from the existence of an endpoint.

Research tasks use explicit timeboxes; effort is counted once in the parent
story's `estimativa`. See [estimation](../../agile/estimation-and-prioritization.md),
[client alignment](../../product/client-alignment.md), and the
[story catalog](../../agile/user-stories.md).
