# 🎤 Presentation

This folder holds the materials used to present **DatumLex** to the professors, evaluators, and the Xertica partner.

## Contents

- **Slide decks** — for sprint reviews, the individual presentation, and the team presentation (both count toward the final grade, per the challenge rules: *Individual Presentation + Team Presentation = Final Grade*).


## Why it matters

Sprint Reviews and the final presentations are graded events, and both the individual and team components matter. Keeping decks and demo scripts here — versioned alongside the rest of the documentation — makes it easy to reuse and build on previous presentations instead of starting from scratch each sprint.
